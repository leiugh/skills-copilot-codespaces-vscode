{# Added: Critical priority tier #}
Critical
  • Fire incidents
  • Flooding or natural disasters
  • Ongoing crimes (robbery, assault, violence)
  • Medical emergencies
  • Gas leaks
  • Electrical fire or exposed live wires
  • Missing persons
  • Building/House collapse
  • Road accidents

Major

  • Water interruption in the community
  • Power outages
  • Illegal dumping of garbage
  • Damaged drainage systems
  • Noise complaints
  • Broken streetlights
{# Added: Minor priority tier #}
Minor

  • Graffiti / Vandalism
  • Overgrown grass / plants
  • Minor noise complaints
  • Broken benches or any public use
  • Request for additional bins
  • Minor sidewalk cracks
