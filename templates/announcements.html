🚨 WEATHER WARNING: TYPHOON PREPAREDNESS ADVISORY

Typhoon Warning: Signal No. 2 has been officially declared for Metro Manila due to an approaching tropical depression. Expect heavy continuous downpours, strong gusty winds, and localized flooding in low-lying areas near the riverbanks.

All residents are strictly advised to remain indoors, secure loose outdoor objects, keep emergency kits (food, flashlights, radios) fully charged and accessible, and closely monitor official alerts.

🏥 CITY HEALTH Caravan: STA. ANA DISTRICT

The City Government of Manila will be launching a comprehensive Mobile Public Health Caravan this coming Saturday, June 6th, 2026, at the Sta. Ana Health Center grounds from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Offered services include free pediatric check-ups, dental extraction, routine diagnostic screenings, basic medical prescriptions, and public single-dose vaccines. Special express lanes will be available for senior citizens, pregnant women, and infants.

💧 URGENT WATER SERVICE INTERRUPTION ADVISORY

Maynilad Water Services has announced emergency leak repair operations on main high-pressure aqueduct pipes. Water supply interruption will take place across Sta. Mesa, Sampaloc, and parts of San Miguel.

The outage will begin on Wednesday, June 3rd, 2026, from 10:00 PM until Thursday, June 4th, 2026, at 6:00 AM. All affected community members are urged to store clean water supplies before the maintenance window.
